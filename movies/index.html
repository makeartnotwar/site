    • THE LAST SPECTRAL SIGHT (Production, ARGENTINA, MEXICO, USA)

    Neorealism film focusing on the plight of Carlos, a Mexican immigrant trying to make it to America. As Carlos finally makes it across the border he encounters Tina, a free spirited biker who gives Carlos two options: face deportation or legalizing his papers by getting married with her in Las Vegas. His decision helps shape the unforgettable road journey that lies ahead.
